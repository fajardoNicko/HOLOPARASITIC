Before You Collapse — 3 Safety Tips

Dehydration · Overexertion · Hyperthermia
Dehydration

Sip, don’t chug.

Losing body water through heavy sweating. Tinikling hopping dries up fluids fast.
Do: small sips of water every 15 minutes of rehearsal — before, during, and after.

Overexertion

Rest between runs.

Pushing muscles and the heart past safe limits — continuous high-speed spins and partner lifts in Ballroom.
Do: listen to your body — dizzy or breathless? Stop and sit down immediately.

Hyperthermia

Keep the air moving.

Dangerously high body temperature from dancing in hot, crowded, unventilated spaces.
Do: open windows, wear lightweight clothing, and take regular cooling breaks.

First Aid: The PRICE Protocol

Twisted ankle · struck by a bamboo pole
P
PROTECT

Guard the injured area — stop the dancing, clear the space.

R
REST

Stop all movement. Let the dancer sit or lie down comfortably.

I
ICE

Apply a wrapped cold pack for 15–20 minutes.

C
COMPRESS

Wrap the joint firmly with an elastic bandage — not too tight.

E
ELEVATE

Prop the injured limb above heart level.

Apply PRICE first — then bring the dancer to the school resources below.
